BlockFall

Simple rules. Endless fun.

Move, rotate and drop the blocks. Complete horizontal rows to clear them and increase your score.

▶ Play Game

How to Play

1

Move

Use ← and → to move the falling block.

2

Rotate

Use ↑ to rotate the block.

3

Drop

Use ↓ to make the block fall faster.

4

Clear

Fill a complete row to remove it and score points.
